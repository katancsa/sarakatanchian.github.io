Applied Macroeconomics · Regional Economics · Econometrics · Bayesian & Spatial Methods · Forecasting

My research studies how national economic shocks generate heterogeneous responses across U.S. regions, sectors, and income groups. I use high-frequency economic data and develop Bayesian spatial dynamic factor, VAR, and forecasting models to understand the geographic and distributional transmission of aggregate shocks.

Research

The Geography of Consumption: Local and Sectoral Responses to National Shocks
with Olivier Parent · Job Market Paper · Submitted manuscript

We develop a Bayesian spatial dynamic factor model to study why common nationwide fluctuations produced sharply different consumer-spending responses across U.S. states and sectors after 2020. The model separates a national factor, spatially correlated state-sector exposure, and sectoral dynamics using weekly card-spending data across 50 states and 10 sectors.

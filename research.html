Job Market Paper

The Geography of Consumption: Local and Sectoral Responses to National Shocks
with Olivier Parent · Submitted manuscript · Presented at the 65th Southern Regional Science Association Annual Meeting, 2026

We develop a Bayesian spatial dynamic factor model to study why common nationwide fluctuations produced sharply different consumer-spending responses across U.S. states and sectors after 2020. Using weekly card spending across ten sectors and fifty states, the model decomposes outcomes into a national factor, spatially correlated state-sector exposures, and sectoral dynamics. Exposure to the national factor is strongly spatially clustered, and apparent cross-sector spillovers largely disappear once the common component is controlled for.
